Mission 2000

WHO CAN JOIN

Mission 2000 welcomes everyone who believes that investing in children is investing in the future.

Become your child’s first teacher and strongest advocate by creating a nurturing, stimulating, and loving environment.

Inspire curiosity, confidence, creativity, and lifelong learning during the years that matter most.

Create learning environments that place children’s holistic development at the centre of education.

Strengthen communities by providing quality care, nutrition, and early learning opportunities.

Support healthy beginnings through maternal care, child health, nutrition, developmental guidance, and family education.

Advance knowledge, innovation, and evidence-based practices that improve outcomes for children.

Champion policies and programmes that strengthen early childhood development nationwide.

Extend support to families, build awareness, and reach vulnerable communities.

Invest in children through Corporate Social Responsibility, workplace family initiatives, research, innovation, and community partnerships.

Contribute your time, skills, ideas, and passion to help children and families flourish.

Every conversation, every act of kindness, every effort to support a child helps build a stronger nation.

Mission 2000

JOIN THE MOVEMENT

Mission 2000 is more than an initiative.

It is a national movement to ensure that every child receives the care, health, nutrition, learning, protection, and opportunities they deserve during the first 2,000 days of life.

This mission cannot be achieved by governments alone.

It cannot be achieved by schools alone.

It cannot be achieved by parents alone.

It requires all of us.

Whether you are an individual, an institution, or an organisation, you have the power to help shape the future of India—one child at a time.

Together, we can build a nation where every beginning is filled with hope, every family feels supported, every educator is respected, and every child is given the opportunity to thrive.

An Invitation to Partner in Nation Building

Dear Partners,

Every meaningful transformation in history has been built through collaboration.

No single school can educate an entire nation.

No single organisation can nurture every child.

No single institution can unlock the full potential of future generations.

It is only when visionary organisations come together with a shared purpose that lasting change becomes possible.

Mission 2026 is our invitation to you to become part of a movement that places children at the heart of national development.

We invite you to join us—not simply as a partner, but as a co-creator of India’s future.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive.

By partnering with Mission 2026, you contribute to an ecosystem that supports:

  • Quality early childhood education.
  • Teacher excellence and professional development.
  • Parent engagement and family learning.
  • Child health, nutrition, and wellbeing.
  • Innovation through science, technology, and responsible AI.
  • Research and evidence-based practice.
  • Inclusive education and equal opportunity.
  • Community development through early childhood investment.

Together, these efforts help create stronger children, stronger families, stronger communities, and a stronger India.

Mission 2026 welcomes collaboration with:

  • Schools and educational institutions.
  • Universities and research organisations.
  • Healthcare providers and hospitals.
  • Government departments and public institutions.
  • Corporate organisations and CSR programmes.
  • Foundations and philanthropic trusts.
  • NGOs and community organisations.
  • Technology and innovation partners.
  • Media and communication organisations.
  • Professional associations.
  • Individual experts, mentors, and volunteers.

Every partner brings unique knowledge, experience, and resources that strengthen the movement.

Every partnership is built upon:

  • Shared Purpose — Children’s wellbeing comes before institutional interests.
  • Integrity — We act with honesty, transparency, and accountability.
  • Respect — We value diverse perspectives and expertise.
  • Innovation — We encourage new ideas that improve children’s lives.
  • Evidence — We support approaches informed by research and continuous learning.
  • Impact — We focus on measurable, meaningful outcomes for children and families.

Partners can make a difference by:

  • Supporting teacher development.
  • Strengthening parent education programmes.
  • Advancing research and innovation.
  • Sharing knowledge and expertise.
  • Sponsoring learning resources and community initiatives.
  • Supporting child health and wellbeing programmes.
  • Creating family-friendly workplace initiatives.
  • Offering technology, skills, mentoring, or professional services.
  • Expanding access to quality early childhood education.

Every contribution, regardless of size, helps create a stronger future.

Mission 2026 is committed to building partnerships based on trust, mutual respect, transparency, and shared impact.

We value long-term relationships that create sustainable change and measurable outcomes for children, families, educators, and communities.

Together, we will celebrate progress, learn from experience, and continually strive for excellence.

Years from now, the true measure of our partnership will not be the number of meetings we held or the projects we completed.

It will be the lives we helped shape.

The children who grew with confidence.

The families who found support.

The teachers who inspired future generations.

The communities that became stronger because we chose to work together.

This is the legacy we have the privilege to build.

By joining Mission 2000, I pledge to:

  • Place children at the heart of every decision.
  • Support parents as a child’s first teachers.
  • Respect and strengthen the role of educators and caregivers.
  • Promote healthy, safe, and nurturing environments.
  • Encourage curiosity, creativity, play, and lifelong learning.
  • Support science, compassion, and evidence-based practice.
  • Work collaboratively across communities and sectors.
  • Champion the rights, dignity, and potential of every child.
  • Inspire others to become part of this movement.
  • Help build a stronger India by giving every child the strongest beginning.

No movement becomes national because of one organisation.

It becomes national because millions of people decide that the cause is larger than themselves.

Mission 2000 belongs to every parent who dreams of a brighter future.

Every teacher who believes in the power of learning.

Every healthcare professional who protects healthy beginnings.

Every policymaker who invests in children.

Every organisation that chooses to serve society.

Every volunteer who gives their time.

Every citizen who believes that India’s greatest resource is its people.

This is your movement.

This is our responsibility.

This is our opportunity.

Mission 2026 is an open invitation to every organisation and every individual who believes that investing in children is investing in the future of humanity.

Together, let us transform today’s opportunities into tomorrow’s possibilities.

Together, let us build a future where every child has the strongest possible beginning.

Mission 2000

FOR TEACHERS

We believe that the greatest builders of a nation are not found only in boardrooms, laboratories, or parliaments.

They are found in classrooms.

Every smile you encourage…

Every question you answer…

Every story you tell…

Every child you comfort…

Every dream you inspire…

Shapes the future of our nation.

Mission 2020 is our shared commitment to become the very best version of ourselves—for every child entrusted to our care.

As educators, we commit ourselves to nurturing children who are:

  • Curious in thought
  • Confident in expression
  • Kind in character
  • Creative in imagination
  • Healthy in body and mind
  • Respectful of others
  • Proud of their identity
  • Ready for lifelong learning

We understand that teaching is not the transfer of knowledge.

It is the awakening of human potential.

Every teacher pledges to:

  • Love Before Learning — Every child deserves to feel safe, respected, accepted, and valued. Children learn best when they know they are loved.
  • Inspire Curiosity — We will encourage children to ask questions, explore, discover, and think independently.
  • Make Learning Joyful — Learning should be filled with play, creativity, music, movement, storytelling, imagination, and meaningful experiences.
  • Know Every Child — We will understand each child’s unique strengths, interests, learning style, personality, and developmental journey.
  • Partner with Parents — Parents are our partners. Together we will work for the happiness, growth, and success of every child.
  • Continue Learning — Great teachers never stop learning. We will continuously improve our knowledge, skills, and professional practice.
  • Lead by Example — Children learn more from what we do than from what we say. Our kindness, patience, honesty, enthusiasm, and integrity become their lessons.
  • Build Character — Success is measured not only by academic achievement but also by empathy, responsibility, resilience, respect, courage, and compassion.
  • Use Innovation Responsibly — We will embrace technology and artificial intelligence as tools that support learning while ensuring that human relationships remain at the heart of education.
  • Celebrate Every Child — Every child deserves encouragement. Every achievement deserves recognition. Every effort deserves appreciation.
  • I pledge to teach with passion.
  • I pledge to lead with compassion.
  • I pledge to inspire curiosity.
  • I pledge to nurture confidence.
  • I pledge to respect every child.
  • I pledge to partner with every family.
  • I pledge to continue learning.
  • I pledge to build character before achievement.
  • I pledge to create joyful classrooms.
  • I pledge to help every child discover their unique potential.

Because I know…

The future of India sits in my classroom every day.

As teachers, we do not simply prepare children for examinations.

We prepare them for life.

We do not merely teach subjects.

We help shape human beings.

We do not only educate minds.

We nurture hearts.

We do not simply change children’s futures.

We help shape the future of our nation.

Mission 2020

Great Children Build Great Nations.
