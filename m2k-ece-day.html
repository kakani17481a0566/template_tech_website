The Launch Of A National Movement

WHY 10 AUGUST?

10 August is more than a date—it is a symbol of new beginnings.

The number 10 represents the final days before a child's birth, reminding us that the foundations of life begin even before birth.
The month of August commemorates India's independence and the birth of a free nation.
Mission 2000 brings these two ideas together: the birth of a child and the birth of a nation's future. By investing in every child's first 2,000 days, we build India's Human Capital and strengthen our National Capability.

Mission 2000

NATIONAL ECEC DAY

Celebrating the Beginning of Every Life

Observed annually on 10 August, this day recognises that the first years of life are not merely the beginning of childhood; they are the foundation upon which learning, health, character, creativity, wellbeing, and lifelong potential are built.

It is a day to honour the parents, families, teachers, healthcare professionals, Anganwadi workers, caregivers, researchers, policymakers, and communities whose dedication shapes these precious early years.

Most importantly, it is a day to reaffirm our shared national commitment that every child deserves the strongest possible beginning.

Mission 2000

WHY 10 AUGUST?

Mission 2000 marks 10 August as the launch of a national movement to place the first 2,000 days of life at the heart of India’s development.

It represents a day of awareness, celebration, learning, collaboration, and renewed commitment to the wellbeing of children.

Mission 2000

WHY EARLY CHILDHOOD EDUCATION MATTERS?

The first years of life are a remarkable period of growth and discovery.

Positive experiences, responsive relationships, good nutrition, safe environments, quality early learning, and opportunities to play and explore all contribute to healthy development.

When families, educators, healthcare professionals, and communities work together, children are better prepared to learn, thrive, and contribute to society.

Investing in early childhood is one of the most meaningful investments a nation can make in its future.

Mission 2000

OBJECTIVES OF NATIONAL ECE DAY

National Early Childhood Education Day seeks to:

  • Celebrate children as the foundation of India’s future.
  • Honour parents as a child’s first teachers.
  • Recognise the invaluable contribution of early childhood educators and caregivers.
  • Encourage collaboration between education, health, nutrition, and community services.
  • Inspire innovation, research, and evidence-informed practice in early childhood development.
  • Strengthen partnerships across governments, institutions, civil society, and industry.
  • Foster a national culture that values childhood, learning, inclusion, and wellbeing.
Mission 2000

WHO WE CELEBRATE

On this day, we celebrate:

  • Every child.
  • Every parent and family.
  • Every early childhood educator.
  • Every Anganwadi worker.
  • Every healthcare professional supporting mothers and young children.
  • Every preschool, childcare centre, and school.
  • Every researcher advancing knowledge.
  • Every volunteer serving children.
  • Every organisation committed to building a brighter future through early childhood.
Mission 2000

HOW INDIA CAN PARTICIPATE

Communities across the country are encouraged to celebrate through activities such as:

  • Child-centred learning and play experiences.
  • Parent education programmes.
  • Teacher recognition ceremonies.
  • Health, nutrition, and wellbeing awareness initiatives.
  • Storytelling, music, art, and cultural celebrations.
  • Public lectures and research forums.
  • Community service projects supporting young children and families.
  • School open days and family engagement events.
  • Conferences, workshops, and policy dialogues.
  • Recognition of outstanding contributions to early childhood education and development.

Every activity, whether large or small, contributes to a shared national purpose: creating the best possible beginning for every child.

Mission 2000

NATIONAL ECE PLEDGE

On this day, we reaffirm our commitment:

  • We will value every child.
  • We will support every family.
  • We will respect every educator.
  • We will protect every child’s right to learn, grow, play, and thrive.
  • We will promote science, compassion, inclusion, and collaboration.
  • We will work together to ensure that the first years of life receive the attention they deserve.

Together, we will build a stronger India by giving every child the strongest possible beginning.
