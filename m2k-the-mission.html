Mission 2000

OUR VISION

To make India the world’s leading nation in early childhood development by ensuring that every child receives the care, nutrition, protection, love, learning, and opportunities needed during the first 2,000 days of life—laying the foundation for a healthier, happier, more capable, and more compassionate society.

Mission 2000 believes that the future of India is not built when children enter university or the workforce.

It is built during the first 2,000 days of life.

Our vision is not merely to improve early childhood education.

Our vision is to transform the future of the nation by transforming the beginning of every life.

Mission 2000

OUR MISSION

Mission 2000 seeks to:

  • Promote awareness of the critical importance of the first 2,000 days.
  • Support parents as a child’s first and most influential teachers.
  • Elevate the status and professional development of early childhood educators.
  • Encourage evidence-based practices grounded in neuroscience, child development, nutrition, psychology, and education.
  • Foster collaboration across public, private, and community sectors.
  • Inspire innovation through research, technology, and responsible use of artificial intelligence.
  • Advocate policies that strengthen children, families, and early learning ecosystems.
  • Build a national culture that celebrates childhood as the foundation of human potential.
Mission 2000

OUR GUIDING BELIEFS

We believe every child deserves the best possible start in life.

We believe parents are a child’s first teachers.

We believe teachers are nation builders.

We believe science and compassion must work together.

We believe early investment creates lifelong impact.

We believe every community shares responsibility for its children.

We believe technology should serve humanity.

We believe collaboration creates lasting change.

We believe that the first 2,000 days shape the next 20,000.

We believe that when children flourish, families flourish.

When families flourish, communities flourish.

When communities flourish, India flourishes.

Mission 2000 is more than a programme.

It is more than a campaign.

It is a national movement.

A movement that places children before systems.

Potential before limitation.

Prevention before intervention.

Learning before testing.

Partnership before isolation.

Hope before fear.
